# Compatibility check of the shared platform

PCPartPicker does not list Kazakhstani shops, so it is used here only to check that the parts fit together, not for prices (prices are in `../budget.csv`).



PCPartPicker link: https://pcpartpicker.com/list/m7D8xf

## Points I checked against the shop listings

|Check|Result|Where to verify|
|-|-|-|
|CPU and board share a socket|AM5 on both|e-katalog.kz listings for the 7600X and B650M-P|
|Board takes DDR5 and has four DIMM slots|yes, 4 x DDR5|Flexcom.kz listing of MSI PRO B650M-P|
|Case takes a micro-ATX board|yes, mATX|e-katalog.kz, Deepcool WAVE V2|
|Cooler fits AM5|yes|ID-Cooling SE-214-XT product page|
|PSU is enough|550 W is plenty for a 7600X with integrated graphics and no GPU|MSI MAG A550BN listing|
|Monitor connects without adapters|board has HDMI and DisplayPort; the monitors have both|board and monitor listings|
